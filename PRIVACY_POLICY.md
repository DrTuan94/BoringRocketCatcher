# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Published at: <https://drtuan94.github.io/BoringRocketCatcher/>

This Privacy Policy explains how **Skibidi Catch** handles information when you use the app on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or tvOS.

## Summary

Skibidi Catch does not collect personal information, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your data.

The app stores gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device. If you use Apple services such as Game Center or in-app purchases, those features are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policies.

## Information We Store Locally

The app may store the following information on your device using Apple-provided local storage:

- High scores and gameplay progress
- Daily challenge progress, streaks, and run counts
- Achievement unlocks
- Selected rocket skin
- Sound, music, and haptic settings
- A local cache of subscription or purchase entitlement status
- First-launch or onboarding state

This information is stored locally for app functionality and is not sent to our own servers.

## Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features, Apple may process information related to your Game Center account, leaderboard scores, display name, and related activity. Game Center is provided by Apple, and your use of Game Center is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

You can manage Game Center from your device's Apple account settings.

## In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Skibidi Catch may offer auto-renewable subscriptions and non-consumable in-app purchases. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, billing, and transaction processing are handled by Apple through the App Store.

The app checks purchase entitlement status through Apple's StoreKit system and may store a local cache of whether an entitlement is active so the app can unlock purchased content correctly.

We do not receive or store your payment card details.

## Tracking and Advertising

Skibidi Catch does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not request permission to track under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information through our own servers, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Parents and guardians can manage Game Center, App Store purchases, and device privacy settings through Apple's parental controls and Family Sharing tools.

## Data Deletion

Because gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device, you can delete this app data by deleting the app from your device. Apple may separately retain information related to App Store purchases, subscriptions, Game Center, or other Apple services according to Apple's own policies.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple platform services, including:

- Game Center, for optional leaderboards
- StoreKit and the App Store, for purchases and subscriptions
- Local device storage, for preferences and gameplay state

Apple's privacy policy is available at <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised policy at the same location.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact listed on the app's App Store product page or through this project's GitHub repository.
